# M4A1 reference analysis

Observed: one complete side-on black carbine against a white background, muzzle forward, adjustable stock rearward. Object domain, mechanical compound assembly. Long axis about 3.1 times total height including magazine. Receiver and handguard share the barrel axis. Magazine projects below the forward receiver with a curved forward edge. The pistol grip projects downward and rearward; the vertical foregrip is below the handguard. Stock has a hollow lower triangular frame and a vented cheek surface. Forward sight is a triangular raised frame. Rear sight is a raised folding block. The top rail is a repeated notched strip.

Macro assemblies: stock/buffer tube; upper and lower receiver; handguard; barrel/muzzle; magazine; pistol grip; vertical foregrip. Meso: sights, trigger guard, charging handle, selector, bolt catch, sling loops, rail strips. Micro: rail teeth, grip ribs, magazine ribs, fastener heads, receiver seams, vent slots.

Attachment: barrel is coaxial with and embedded in handguard; handguard overlaps receiver front; buffer tube sockets into rear receiver; stock slides over tube; magazine inserts into lower receiver; grip joins behind trigger guard. Sights attach above rail. No part should float away from the receiver.

Observed finish: receiver and barrel dark charcoal satin, handguard/grips darker matte, magazine lighter gray with longitudinal bands. Inferred material assignment: coated metal receiver/barrel (moderate roughness), polymer stock/grips (dielectric, high roughness), metallic magazine with muted highlights. These are visual inferences, not measured PBR values. Source pixels remain reference-only and will not ship as textures.

Critical identity: open trigger guard and stock frame, curved magazine, cylindrical stepped barrel, raised triangular front sight, top rail cadence, ribs on foregrip, lower receiver/grip relationship. Single-view uncertainty: opposite-side ejection port, hidden widths, internal mechanism and exact markings are not visible; these must be reported as inferred. Socket positions are animation affordances, not manufacturing coordinates.

Suitability: conditional pass for browser procedural reconstruction, complete unclipped outline and clear parts; not sufficient for exact hidden-side geometry. User authorized continued reference use. Target 12000 triangles, silhouette comparison >=0.70; no score yet measured. Review side, opposite side, front and rear plus hip-fire and ADS framing.
